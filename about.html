AI conversation and profile coaching

Wingman / MyWingman is an AI-powered dating-conversation and profile coaching software service for adults aged 18 and over. It helps you explore reply suggestions, analyze conversation screenshots, generate icebreakers, improve dating-profile bios, and practice conversations with AI coaching.

The tools support your own communication and judgment. Wingman does not match users, provide profiles of potential partners, introduce strangers or arrange dates. AI suggestions can be inaccurate, and matches, dates or relationships are never guaranteed.

You can use the available complimentary signup credits to explore the workspace. Paid bundles are one-time purchases; paid checkout is currently unavailable in production.
