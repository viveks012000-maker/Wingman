Practise a clear invitation

“I’m planning to check out the market on Saturday. Would you be interested in joining me? No worries if your weekend is full.”

Maeve conversation coach questions

Is Maeve a real person?

No. Maeve is a fictional AI conversation practice partner inside the MyWingman workspace. She does not have a dating profile or contact other people.

Can Maeve send a message to my match?

No. Practice stays in the MyWingman workspace. You choose whether to write or send anything yourself through your own app.

What does a practice turn cost?

The workspace currently displays a 2-credit charge per Practice Partner & Coach message turn. Other tools have different credit costs.

Need help with a real draft?

Practice is useful for exploring a situation. For a message already on your screen, use screenshot replies; for a first message, create a profile-based icebreaker.
