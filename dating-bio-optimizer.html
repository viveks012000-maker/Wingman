What a dating bio optimizer should improve

A useful rewrite should help a reader understand something real about you and give them an easy conversation opening. The goal is not to invent a more impressive personality or make promises about matches. Start with details you can stand behind: an activity you enjoy, a small preference or the kind of time you like sharing.

MyWingman offers four rewrite directions: Punchy, Playful, Green Flag and Mysterious. Compare the drafts with your original and keep only details that are true to your life.

A practical editing checklist

KEEP / TRUE

Check every claim

Remove any hobby, preference or life detail the AI added if it is not genuinely yours.

MAKE / SPECIFIC

Swap vague words for details

“I like travel” becomes clearer when you name a place, a kind of trip or a small thing you enjoy doing there.

INVITE / CONVERSATION

Leave room for a reply

A simple question or shared-interest prompt can make a bio easier to respond to without demanding attention.

Dating bio optimizer questions

How long can my dating bio be?

The MyWingman bio field accepts up to 500 words. You can paste a shorter draft and compare the generated rewrites.

Will the bio optimizer create facts about me?

AI can suggest wording and may introduce details that do not fit you. Review every draft carefully and delete anything you cannot honestly claim.

Does it guarantee more matches?

No. The tool generates profile wording ideas. It cannot predict who will see or respond to a profile, and it does not promise dating outcomes.
