Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does MyWingman analyze my chat screenshots?

MyWingman analyzes text context from an uploaded screenshot and suggests possible replies in your selected tone. AI output can be inaccurate; review and edit it before use.

Does MyWingman send messages for me?

No. MyWingman generates suggestions only; it does not send messages through dating apps or contact other users. You decide whether to edit or use any suggestion.

Does the engine understand localized slang and emojis?

AI may misunderstand slang, humor, or context. Review every suggestion and rely on your own judgment before using it.

Can I use these credits for profile audits, icebreakers, or Practice Partner & Coach chat too?

When paid checkout is enabled, credits will be shared across available tools. Screenshot analysis, bio optimization, and icebreaker requests use 10 credits each; a practice chat reply turn uses 2 credits.

Do my purchased credits expire?

No. All credits purchased in MyWingman are non-expiring credits. Use them whenever you need to analyze a chat screenshot or generate icebreakers.
