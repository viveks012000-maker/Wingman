What a reply draft can look like

Suppose the visible message says:

“I judge a café by its pastries and whether the chairs are comfortable.”

A light, curious draft might be:

“Pastries are the important test. What’s your order at a place that gets it right?”

The example is written for this page. It is not a real user conversation or a promise that a reply will get a particular response.

Useful context—and a few limits

The analyzer drafts wording from visible screenshot context. It may miss sarcasm, cropped text, app-specific details or what happened outside the image, so treat each option as a draft rather than a reading of someone’s intentions.

Submitted screenshots are sent to third-party AI processors for analysis. Only upload content you are allowed to share, and see the Privacy Policy for the processing and retention details. The tool does not contact another person, access your dating account or send messages.

Screenshot reply analyzer questions

Which dating apps does it work with?

The analyzer starts from an image you upload, so it is not connected to a particular dating app. It can use readable conversation text in a screenshot; text that is blurred, too small, covered or cropped may not be interpreted correctly.

Does MyWingman send the reply?

No. It provides suggestions in the workspace. You decide whether to copy, edit or discard them and send any message through your own app.

How many credits does one analysis use?

The workspace currently shows a 10-credit charge for one screenshot analysis before you run it. Credit use is separate from any response another person may send.
