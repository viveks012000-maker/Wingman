Digital service delivery

Wingman is a digital software service. No physical product is shipped, and no shipping or courier applies. Successful credit-bundle purchases provide digital Wingman credits for use in your signed-in account.

Credits normally appear immediately after successful verified payment. A cancelled, failed or unverified payment does not grant purchased credits. Paid checkout is currently unavailable in production.

If your purchased credits are missing

Contact support.mywingman@gmail.com with your account email, payment date, amount, currency and transaction reference. Support will investigate and either provide the correctly purchased credits or process an eligible refund within 5–7 days or earlier.

Approved refunds return to the original payment method used for purchase, under our Cancellation & Refund Policy. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to display a processed refund.
